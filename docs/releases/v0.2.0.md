## What's new in v0.2.0

The outstation now works alongside the **Outside plugin** for RuneLite, on RuneLite's Plugin Hub. Each one does its own part: the plugin puts what you wear and carry, your stats, your bank and more on your Outside page, and the outstation transmits your game and keeps your clips.

### Before you update

- **Dink no longer goes through the outstation.** The outstation keeps Dink's posts only to save clips. In RuneLite, put these in Dink's **Primary Webhook URLs**, each on a line of its own:
  - the outstation's own address (**dink ▸** shows it), so clips still save themselves;
  - your Discord webhook, if you want Dink's posts in Discord;
  - the Outside plugin's Dink address (its side tab ▸ More ▸ **Copy my Dink address**), for your field reports on your page.

  If your outstation used to pass Dink's posts on, it tells you this once, and it keeps your Discord webhook for your clips.
- **Who sees your page** is set in the Outside plugin now (its side tab ▸ More ▸ **Who sees it**: anyone, or friends only). The outstation follows it.
- **Your year** is kept by the Outside plugin now, and the outstation no longer records one. If yours kept a year before, **settings ▸ more ▸ your old year record** opens it or deletes it from this PC.
- The station log is now `outstation.log` (it was `portal.log`). It's moved over by itself.

### New

- **A first-time setup in the order things need:** the Outside plugin and your station key for it (pasted in the plugin's settings ▸ Advanced), then the clips, Dink, Discord and the Start menu. Each step can be skipped. **settings ▸ more** runs it again.
- **Invites:** issue station keys to friends from the friends drawer. You start with three, and get one more for every ten hours you transmit. Each invite says who it's from.
- **The aether:** a tab in the chat with one room for everyone on Outside.
- **Your own emotes:** add pictures in **settings ▸ emotes**. They show in your page's chat, and now in the outstation's chat too.
- **clips ▸:** the recovered footage on your page (watch it, file up to 3 for good, take any down) and the newest clips on this PC. A pet and its collection log slot now make one clip, not two.
- **Clips while dark:** "and while dark too" keeps the tape rolling between transmissions, and nothing is sent. Or choose **clips only ▸** on the first screen if you only want the clips.
- **The sound stops with the picture** (on to begin with): no sound while RuneLite is minimised, closed or logged out. **Never the screen** (off to begin with): if RuneLite's own picture can't be read, nothing is sent rather than your screen.
- **The chat over the game fades** after a while (your choice of how long). With **chat in the picture**, it's drawn into what observers and your clips see.
- **Nobody watching, almost nothing goes out:** with no one in a seat, the outstation sends one frame every 10 seconds and no sound, then the full stream the moment someone arrives.
- **Pings** now spread out and fade smoothly.

### Fixed

- Going live no longer drops friends who are watching a friends-only page.
- Attaching a station key never starts a transmission by itself, and **go dark** now cancels a transmission that's still waiting for the relay.
- **Esc** no longer closes the outstation while you're typing.
- Friends' pages are found by their names as the game writes them now (Oran Berries is `oran_berries`).
- The sound stops retrying when the relay turns it away, and opens nothing while it's off.
- A settings file that can't be read is set aside, and your station key is never lost to it.
- Opening the outstation with RuneLite already open no longer freezes it for seconds.
- The station log stays under 2 MB, even with the window open for days.
- Many smaller fixes to wording and to what each screen says.

Something wrong? The pink **report an anomaly** button at the foot of the window sends it to the listening post.
