# Every boss, raid and boss-type minigame, and how the encounter recorder takes it (11/22/2026)

The encounter recorder (`Encounters.java`) starts and ends a recording by itself at every boss, raid and boss-type minigame the game has. This is its table (`EncounterBosses.java`, read by the recorder for each decision) written up: one row each, where it is, whether it's an instance, its boss's names, its count line, how the fight really ends, what the recorder did before this audit and what it does now. `EncounterBossesTest.java` walks every row through the recorder's decisions against its own list of what each should do.

**Where each fact comes from.** Nothing from memory:
- **Areas and regions:** RuneLite's Discord plugin (`DiscordGameEventType`, runelite/runelite master of 11/18/2026), the same list `Presence.java` holds. For a boss Discord has no area for, the OSRS Wiki's map pins (its LocLine x and y as regions) are given here for reference only: the recorder places those bosses by their fight, not by a region.
- **NPC names and ids:** the game's cache (OpenRS2's cache 2772, revision 242, 11/18/2026), its NPC configs read in Node with the replay's `defs.js`; ids named after RuneLite's `gameval/NpcID`. Names are matched in any case.
- **Count lines:** RuneLite's chat commands (`ChatCommandsPlugin`'s `KILLCOUNT_PATTERN`, its boss-name table, and the lines its `ChatCommandsPluginTest` quotes: "RL" below) and Dink (pajlads/DinkPlugin's `KillCountNotifier` and its tests: "Dink"). "RL's name" means RuneLite's chat commands keep the count under that name, which is the line's own, in the game's one form "Your <name> kill count is: <n>."; "hiscores" means only the hiscores' name is known, the line isn't quoted anywhere read, and any count line ends it.
- **How a fight goes:** the OSRS Wiki's pages, cited in the rows and in `EncounterBosses.java`.

**Counts.** 70 rows: 32 of Discord's boss areas (2 of them skilling, never recorded), its 6 raid areas (2 a lobby or a part), 7 of its minigames that are a boss fight (Barrows, the Inferno, the Fight Cave, the two Gauntlets, the Colosseum and its lobby), 15 bosses Discord has no area for (14 started by their fight, and Doom of Mokhaiotl by its forms) and 10 in the Wilderness (never). By how they start: on entering 6 (4 raids, the Inferno, the Fight Cave); an instance on entering, its open copy with the fight 30; with the fight always 15 (Barrows and the 14); by their own rule 4 (the Gauntlets, the Colosseum, Doom); never 15. By how they end: one kill 41, the last of several 1 (the Dagannoth Kings), the last wave's line 3 (the Inferno, the Fight Cave, TzHaar-Ket-Rak), a chest 2 (Barrows, the Moons), a run 3, a raid 4, a delve 1, never 15.

**What changed.** 21 rows: the Inferno, the Fight Cave and Barrows (nothing recorded them: Discord names them minigames); the 14 bosses with no area of Discord's (nothing recorded them by themselves); the Dagannoth Kings (a king's line ended it with his brothers fought and up); the Kalphite Queen (her first form's fall ended it); the Kraken (a tentacle's death counted); the Royal Titans (a corpse could count as a titan still up). And for every row: a kill or death already due that's followed by leaving within its few ticks ends as itself, not "left"; an agility lap's, the Agility Arena's or the herbiboar's count line is no kill.
