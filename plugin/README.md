# Outside, the RuneLite plugin

<!-- hub:cut live -->
What you wear and carry, live on your Outside page: your equipment, inventory, looting bag, rune pouch and Dizana's quiver, your hitpoints, prayer, run and special, the prayers you have on, and every skill's level and experience. It sends them to your outstation's channel on the relay, the same one your transmission and its chat go through, and your page draws them beside them: live while you play, as last seen while you're away.

It works with or without the outstation program. With both, your page has your transmission, its chat and your loadout together. With only the plugin, it has your loadout.

Since 11/16/2026 it also does what the Plugin Hub's version does (below: "Update my page", the hourly sync and what you're doing as Discord shows it); the Hub's version is only that (`hub_cut.py`, "live").

## Setting it up

1. Install **Outside** from the Plugin Hub (RuneLite's wrench icon, then Plugin Hub; not there yet: see "Trying it before the Hub" below).
2. Log in, open the **Outside** side tab (a lime ✼) and press **Make my Outside page** (11/15/2026). Your page is made under the name you're logged in as, at Outside.wiki/<your name>, and its station key goes into the plugin's settings by itself. Until it's proven yours, the page marks the name **unproven**: gain some experience, stop, press **Update my page**, then log out once the side tab says ready (11/17/2026: the update's own answer says so), and within a few minutes the official hiscores show what the plugin reported before they did, which proves it (docs/protocol.md, "Proving a name"). The side tab says which it is. A name someone else claimed first but never proved can be taken back: the side tab says so, you gain experience, stop, press Update my page and log out once the side tab says ready, and once the hiscores prove it yours the page is yours and their key stops working (docs/protocol.md, "Taking a name back"). A proven name, or one an outstation or invite vouches for, can't be taken.
<!-- hub:cut live -->
   - Have an outstation or a friend's invite instead? Paste its **station key** (it starts `outstation1:`) in the plugin's settings.
<!-- hub:end live -->
   - **More than one character** (11/15/2026): each has a page and a key of its own. A page made in the side tab keeps its key with that character's account, so logging into another shows that one's page (or the button to make one). Nothing is sent on a key whose page isn't the character logged in: an alt never shows on a main's page, even with the main's key in the settings.
<!-- hub:cut live -->
3. The game's chat and the side tab say "your Outside page is live: Outside.wiki/<your name>".

## Encounters (11/21/2026)

ghillie's claude/outstation.md, "Encounters on Outside: the night's design": Encounter Recorder (by Mr Gnome, BSD 2-Clause, on the Plugin Hub) folded into this plugin, so a player needs only Outside, writing its file format so both replays read both plugins' files, and the fixes for the gaps found in it (outstation.md, "The code read" and "The test fight") made here, to be offered back to him as pull requests.

**Where the files go.** `.runelite/plugin-data/outside/encounters/` (RuneLite's `getPluginDirectory()`, which needs `internalName = "outside"` in the plugin's descriptor: the Hub's rules let a plugin's files go only through `Filepath`, under its own folder, so not `.runelite/outside/encounters` as first planned). Named as his are (`yyyyMMdd-HHmmss-label.jsonl.gz`, a label already there numbered), written plain while they run on a thread of their own and gzipped when they stop with `{"ticks":N}` in the gzip header's comment; one a crash or a kill left plain is given its end line (`"not stopped: the client closed or crashed"`) and packed the next time the plugin starts, once nothing's written to it for 2 minutes. The newest are kept within "Keep up to (MB)" (200), the oldest packed ones going first; `index.json` beside them keeps each one's summary for the side tab and what's on the page from it.

**Starting.** With "Record my encounters" on (to begin with) and an Outside page for the character: at a boss's or a raid's area as RuneLite's Discord plugin names them (`Presence.AREAS`, kinds boss and raid, an instance by its template; not Wintertodt or Tempoross, skilling), the tick they're first there; and, as his auto-record list, an NPC listed in "Also for these NPCs" appearing, or another NPC turning into one (his never started on a form change). After a kill or a death in an area, the next one there starts with the next fight (a hitsplat on them, or theirs on an NPC); one stopped by hand there doesn't start again till they've left. By hand: **Record now** in the Encounters tab, named after the area or the strongest NPC about, as his. Never in the Wilderness, a PvP area or on a PvP or Deadman world (`OutsidePlugin.held`, the snapshot's rule): none starts there, and one running stops the tick they're found there, that tick's lines dropped.

**Ending.** By the kill: the game's kill or completion count line (`Encounters.KILL_LINE`, the same as `Counts`), 5 ticks later for the death animation and the drop ("killed"), or a few ticks after a chest's or corpse's loot (round three, below). By their death, 6 ticks later ("died"), but not in a raid, which goes on. **A raid** (round three) is one recording from entering to leaving its treasure room: no count line, room or death inside it ends it; once it's completed or looted, its first ticks out of the raid end it ("completed"). By leaving: an area of another name at once, a region no area names after 2 ticks (a raid's, 50: a room the list misses, a map loading) ("left"); for the listed NPCs, his rule, none seen for 50 ticks, a listed NPC that changed form still counting ("watched NPCs gone"). By hand, logging out or hopping, the plugin off, the client closing (it waits for the packing, as his). At 5,000 ticks a part ends ("split: the length limit") and the next part starts at once with its own starting state (`"part"`, `"follows"`), where his stopped.

**What's added to his format** (format 6 kept, so his replay reads these files; what's new is in fields and line kinds a reader that doesn't know them passes over):

| What | How |
|---|---|
| Whose recorder | the header's `"recorder": "outside"`, `"recorderVersion"`, `"player"` (the player's own name, so the side tab offers it only to that character's page), `"area"` (what started it), and for a split `"part"`, `"follows"` |
| The starting state | every varp that isn't 0 as a `var` line (`varbit` -1, the whole value, as his lines give a varp's change), the ground items already lying in the scene as `item` lines, the graphics on tiles as `gfx` lines and projectiles in flight as `proj` lines, each `"init": true` as his start snapshot marks its own |
| An animation restarted | an `anim` line with `"restart": true`, from the actor's frame going back each client cycle while the animation's the same and short of its last frame (RuneLite posts nothing when the same animation is sent again) |
| An object's animation | `obj_anim` (new): `id`, `kind`, `anim` and the tile, when an object within 32 tiles gets a new animated renderable (the server's animation on an object already there; RuneLite posts nothing) |
| A projectile over in a cycle | the client's projectile list read each client cycle too, any not seen written as his `proj` line (one gone before it moves never posts ProjectileMoved) |
| The raids' completion lines | `chat` lines of type `FRIENDSCHATNOTIFICATION`, only a completion or a time ("raid is complete", "Team size", "Duration", "Personal best", "completion time", "Challenge complete"), every other name the client knows (the scene's, the friends chat's) as `<player>` |

Left out on purpose: mouse presses, clicks and their screen places, the interface layout and widgets (`press`, `click`, `ui_layout`, `ui_widgets`), markers, hotkeys and his REC overlay (the Hub's rule against overlays; a replay doesn't need them; the page's strip takes them out anyway). His `dest` lines stay, without the click they came from.

**Its scene moment (11/21/2026, round two).** `EncounterScene` takes what the client draws round the kill and `EncounterMoment` writes it in ghillie's moment format (replay/3d/MOMENT-FORMAT.md, version 2: the still scene's dots once, then each moving model's dots frame by frame, the ground items' names, the loot beams), the same steps as the browser's moment.js `captureStretch` in our own Java. The stretch is moment.js's `stretchFor` in the recorder's terms: 6 ticks before the kill (the count line the recorder ends on) to 5 after it or after the drop (the first item to land within 12 tiles from a tick before the kill to 20 after; the recording waits up to 12 ticks after a kill for one, and 6 past one that lands, where it ended 5 after the kill before), held on the drop; the subject's fall instead when they died after the boss's kill or with none (6 before their death to 5 after, held a tick in, `fall` in the meta); else round the last hit. A rolling buffer keeps the moving things of the last 9 ticks, a frame every 2 client cycles: each NPC's, player's (others only with "Players around you"), projectile's, graphic's, ground item's and animated object's model as the client poses it (`getModel()`), turned and placed as the client's Model drawing does (x' = z sin + x cos, z' = z cos - x sin), its corners kept as whole units (6 bytes a corner), its faces, colours and textures copied once while it stays the same model. At the kill (else at the end) the still scene: tile paints and tile models within 17 tiles on the player's plane and those under it (no roofs), and every still wall, wall decoration, floor decoration and game object by its own model; read off the client's thread. Colours as the replay's view lights them (the client's palette at brightness 0.8, the corners blended; textured faces their texel times their light; see-through texels no dot). Ground items' labels are RuneLite's Ground Items plugin's (ItemManager's GE price, the HA price, QuantityFormatter, its colour tiers, the loot beam over 1M). Fitted to 2.9 MB as moment.js's fit. Written beside the recording as `<file>.moment`, deleted with it.

**Putting one on the page.** `EncounterStrip` is ghillie's `replay/strip.js` (its `strip()`, `summary()` and `foes()`) in Java, reading and writing each line as JavaScript's `JSON.parse` and `JSON.stringify` do (numbers as doubles written as JavaScript writes them, array-index keys first, only JavaScript's escapes: Gson's own writer would put `\u003c` for `<`), so the plugin sends exactly what the page would from the same file. Then gzipped and sent as fights.js sends one (docs/protocol.md, "Recorded fights": the plugin as a sender).

## Where it lives (11/10/2026)

Its root holds `runelite-plugin.properties` and the BSD 2-Clause `LICENSE`, as the Hub asks. Its README is not this one: `README.hub.md` becomes its `README.md`, for players only, and `CONTRIBUTING.hub.md` its `CONTRIBUTING.md`, the build and the source (11/16/2026, the reviewer on runelite/plugin-hub#20811: the README is published on RuneLite's website at the pinned commit, so nothing about the review, the submission or building, and never the outstation or its download). python3 plugin/hub_cut.py ../outside-plugin
